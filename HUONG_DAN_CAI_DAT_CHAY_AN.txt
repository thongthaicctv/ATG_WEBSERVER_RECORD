HUONG DAN CAI DAT ATG_WEBSERVER CHAY AN CUNG WINDOWS

1. Chuan bi thu muc cai dat
- Tao mot thu muc co dinh tren may chay WebServer, vi du:
  D:\PYTHON-TCR\EXE\ATG_WEBSERVER
- Copy cac file sau vao cung thu muc:
  ATG_WEBSERVER.exe
  webserver_config.json

2. Cau hinh WebServer
- Mo ATG_WEBSERVER.exe.
- Dang nhap bang tai khoan root hoac admin co quyen cau hinh.
- Vao menu Cai dat WebServer.
- Nhap thong tin database MySQL/MariaDB.
- Host chay WebServer nen de 0.0.0.0.
- Port nhap port can dung, vi du 8088.
- Neu truy cap bang DDNS, nhap ten mien vao DDNS / Link public.
- O Thu muc goc luu video, nhap moi thu muc mot dong, vi du:
  E:\DEBUG
  D:\VIDEO
  D:\ATG_DataBackup
- Bam Luu cau hinh.

3. Bat che do tu khoi dong an
- Vao menu Tu khoi dong Windows.
- Bat Tu khoi dong ATG_WEBSERVER cung Windows.
- Bat Chay thu nho khi khoi dong.
- Bam Luu cai dat.

4. Kiem tra
- Thoat app bang icon tray: click trai hoac click phai icon ATG_WEBSERVER, chon Exit WebServer.
- Mo lai ATG_WEBSERVER.exe hoac restart Windows.
- Khi chay dung, app khong hien cua so, chi co icon tray va thong bao WebServer dang chay.
- Double click icon tray de mo WebServer.
- Click trai hoac click phai icon tray de mo menu Mo WebServer / Exit WebServer.

5. Luu y khi doi thu muc cai dat
- Neu copy ATG_WEBSERVER.exe sang thu muc khac, phai mo lai trang Tu khoi dong Windows va bam Luu cai dat de cap nhat shortcut/registry.
- Neu doi duong dan luu video trong phan mem record, them thu muc moi vao o Thu muc goc luu video, moi thu muc mot dong.

6. Link truy cap
- http://127.0.0.1:PORT chi dung tren chinh may dang chay WebServer.
- May khac trong LAN dung http://IP_MAY_WEBSERVER:PORT.
- Truy cap ngoai mang dung DDNS/public IP va can cau hinh NAT port tren router.
